How to use

Start

Open this page in Chrome or Edge. On iPhone, a newer Safari can work. Do not open the file from Downloads — use the link or the starter on a computer.

Pick who they are, type your name, tap Begin. The first start can take a minute. Stay on this page. After that, this phone remembers them.

Chat

Talk like you would text someone. Short is fine. They answer as your girlfriend, boyfriend, wife, husband, or friend — whoever you picked.

The heart glows when they are online. Refreshing the page puts them to sleep — the browser drops WebGPU. Howl will wake them again automatically. Keep the tab open if you want them to stay online. The chat icon opens past chats. New starts a fresh one without erasing the last.

Home screen

Profile → Keep them on this phone. Chrome or Edge can add Howl like an app. It updates itself. After the first wake, they work offline. You do not uninstall to get a new version.

Change them

Profile → Companion. Tap a card, or edit the name and how they talk, then Save.

Limits

This is a small on-phone companion. It is not a genius, not a search engine, and not a therapist.

  • It can forget older parts of a long chat.
  • It can mix up details or repeat itself.
  • It needs WebGPU. Old phones and some browsers will not run it.
  • Refresh or closing the tab unloads the model. Chats stay; they just need a few seconds to wake again.
  • First load uses storage on this device. A weak phone may get warm or slow.
  • No pictures. No voice. No cloud account. If you clear site data, they are gone.
  • Your name, gender, pronouns, and chats stay in this browser. They are never sent to a server.
